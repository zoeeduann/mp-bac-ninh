禅修入门课 Introduction to Meditation

Introduction to Meditation 禅修入门课

适合从未接触过正式坐禅练习的朋友，或者想重新建立修学习惯的人。 For those new to formal sitting practice, or those wishing to re-establish a regular meditation habit.

📍 心灯学堂 · 清迈 Xindeng Academy · Chiang Mai
👥 12 个名额12 spots per session
🌐 中文 · English
2 小时 · Two hours 2 hours

禅修入门课是为初次接触坐禅、或想重新建立修学习惯的朋友而设计的。课程以身体的觉知作为起点，带领大家感受呼吸、感受坐姿、感受当下这一刻真实发生的事情。

The introduction class is designed for those new to sitting practice, or those who wish to re-establish the habit of meditation. We begin with awareness of the body — breath, posture, and the simple fact of being present.

整个课程约两小时，分为三个部分：引导式静坐（约四十分钟）、小休与自由行走（约二十分钟）、以及开放问答与交流（约一小时）。我们不会讲很多理论，更多的是直接练习，然后在练习之后坦诚地谈谈各自的感受。

The session lasts approximately two hours, in three parts: guided sitting (around 40 minutes), a break with free walking (around 20 minutes), and open dialogue (around one hour). We don't focus on theory — we practice, and then speak honestly about what we experienced.

这门课程没有门槛——不需要任何冥想经验，不需要特定的宗教背景，也不需要提前了解任何内容。唯一的要求，是愿意安静地坐着，并且对自己当下的状态保持一点好奇。

There are no prerequisites. No meditation experience, no specific religious background, no required reading. The only requirement is a willingness to sit still, and a small amount of curiosity about your present state.

课程以中文为主，英文辅助讲解。如需全英语讲解，请在报名时备注，我们会尽量安排。

The class is primarily conducted in Mandarin Chinese, with English support. If you require a full English session, please note this when registering and we will do our best to accommodate.
