学堂之意 / The Academy The Academy · 学堂之意

心灯学堂坐落于清迈，是一处与日常修学相伴的安静空间。我们不教授什么，只是一起静坐、喝茶、读书、走路。来访的人会发现，这里没有规则，只有一种不疾不徐的节奏。

Nestled at the foot of the mountains in Chiang Mai, Xindeng Academy is a quiet space for daily practice. We don't teach anything — we simply sit together, drink tea, read, and walk. Visitors find that there are no rules here, only a gentle, unhurried rhythm.
